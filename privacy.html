Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information MAVOX LABS ("we", "us") collects when you connect a social media account (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, or similar — each a "Platform") to our publishing software (the "Service"), and how that information is used.

1. Information we collect

  • Authorization tokens. When you connect a Platform account, the Platform issues an OAuth access token (and, where applicable, a refresh token) that lets the Service publish content to that account. We store these tokens only to operate the Service on your behalf.
  • Basic account identifiers. Platforms may share a user ID, display name, and similar basic profile information needed to identify which account is connected.
  • Content you provide. Video files, captions, and metadata you submit to be published are processed solely to complete that publishing action.

We do not collect this information from anyone other than the account owner who authorizes the connection, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

2. How we use this information

Information is used exclusively to operate the Service: authenticating to the Platform's API, publishing the content you submit, and reporting back the result (success or error) of that publishing action.

3. How we share this information

We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is transmitted only to the Platform(s) you have explicitly connected, as required to publish your content, and only in accordance with each Platform's own API terms.

4. Data retention and deletion

Authorization tokens are retained only for as long as an account remains connected. You can revoke access at any time from the Platform's own account settings, which invalidates the associated token. You may also request deletion of any data we hold about your connected account by contacting us at the address below.

5. Security

Authorization tokens and account data are stored using access controls intended to prevent unauthorized access, and are used only by the Service itself to perform the publishing actions you request.

6. Children's privacy

The Service is intended for use by account owners who meet the minimum age requirements of the Platforms they connect, and is not directed at children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be sent to stringmymail@gmail.com.
